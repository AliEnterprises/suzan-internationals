How a booking moves through our desk

The same five stages apply whether it's a pilgrimage group or an export shipment.

01

Consultation

We scope the trip or shipment — dates, group size, destination, and any documentation already in hand.

02

Documentation

Visas, attestations, permits, or customs paperwork are prepared and verified before anything is booked.

03

Booking

Flights, hotels, transport, or freight are confirmed and a manifest is issued with your reference number.

04

Travel / Transit

Your group travels or your goods move, with our team tracking progress and handling any changes.

05

Support

On-ground and after-hours support until the trip ends or the shipment clears at destination.
